# Answers to some questions you might have...

## Why not a normal slideshow?
Hello! The answer to the first question is simple, I really don't like traditional slideshows, they infuriate me to make, and I don't think they look great. I am much more comfortable with web-technology, and it allows for much more flexibility and fun. With the advent of AI as well, iterating quickly with a technology I know well makes much more sense than iterating slowly with a technology I do not.
